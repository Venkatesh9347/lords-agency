Process

How we build.

You always know what stage you're at, what happens next, and what it costs.

  1. 01

    Enquiry

    You send a project brief. We review it carefully and confirm whether we're a good fit — including telling you if you shouldn't build.

  2. 02

    Discovery

    A focused call (or two) to map your process, users, systems and success criteria. Complex work gets a paid discovery with a written spec.

  3. 03

    Architecture, design & build

    System architecture first, then short build iterations with demos on real data. Each phase is confirmed with fixed scope, timeline, price and ownership.

  4. 04

    Testing & QA

    Structured functional testing, validation and review before production release. Testing depth is matched to the system's scope, integrations, security requirements and risk.

  5. 05

    Launch & handover

    Deployment, data migration, access setup, documentation and team training. You own the code and data.

  6. 06

    Support & evolution

    Maintenance, monitoring and iterative improvements. Ongoing support is available as a dedicated arrangement.

FAQ

Straight answers.

Starting a project

How do we start?

Send a project brief below. We review it, and if there's a fit we schedule a discovery call, then send a written proposal with scope, timeline and price.

What do you need from us to give an accurate quote?

The brief below covers it: what you're trying to achieve, your current stage, timeline, and how the process works today. Screenshots, sample files or a short screen recording help a lot.

When can we contact LORDS AGENCY?

Project enquiries are welcome 24/7. We typically respond within 24 hours and can arrange the appropriate discovery or technical discussion based on the enquiry.

Cost & timeline

How much does custom software cost?

It depends on scope, integrations and timeline. Small internal tools and MVPs cost far less than multi-role SaaS platforms. After discovery you get a fixed, phased quote — never an open-ended meter.

How do you price projects?

Pricing is project-specific and depends on project type, scope, complexity, integrations, security requirements and delivery needs. Engagement can be fixed-scope, phased or monthly depending on the project.

Do you offer a first-project discount?

First-project pricing is agreed per engagement rather than published as a fixed discount. Applicable terms are set out in the proposal and confirmed in writing before work begins.

How long does a typical project take?

A focused MVP or internal tool is typically weeks to a few months. Larger platforms are phased so you get usable releases early. Your proposal includes milestones, not just a final date.

Technical

Do we own the code and data?

Yes. You own the codebase, documentation and data. We hand over repositories, credentials and admin access at launch.

Can you take over our existing system?

Yes — that's a dedicated service. We audit the codebase, stabilise critical issues, document it, then continue development. If the system isn't worth saving, we'll tell you before you spend more.

What if we already have software?

We can improve, maintain, integrate or take over existing applications. Anything outside the agreed scope is discussed and agreed with you before it begins.

Can you integrate with tools we already use?

In most cases, yes — CRMs, payment providers, messaging, spreadsheets and internal databases via APIs, webhooks or scheduled sync. Discovery confirms what's technically possible.

How do you test software quality?

Projects include structured functional testing, validation and review before production release. Testing depth is matched to the system's scope, integrations, security requirements and risk.

Working together

Do you provide support after launch?

Yes. Maintenance, fixes, small improvements and training are part of our Support & Training and Maintenance services, or a monthly dedicated arrangement.

Do you sign NDAs?

LORDS AGENCY can work under an NDA when required for a project. Confidential project information should be handled according to the agreed confidentiality terms.

What happens when a project is completed or handed over?

You own the codebase, documentation and data. Project handover can include source code, documentation, deployment information, credentials/access transfer where applicable, and agreed project data. Handover details are defined as part of the engagement.
